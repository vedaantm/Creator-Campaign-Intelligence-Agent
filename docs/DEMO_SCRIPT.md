## Click-by-Click Presenter Script

### Minute 0:00 - 1:00 — Introduction & Campaign Cockpit
- **Action**: Open browser and navigate to `/demo`.
- **Narration**:
  > "Welcome to the Creator Campaign Intelligence Agent (CCIA). Marketers spend millions on influencer campaigns, but suffer from wasted audience overlap, hidden brand safety risks, FTC fines, and missed search demand. CCIA is an end-to-end AI agent that plans, simulates, audits, and monitors influencer campaigns in real time. Let’s look at our Q4 launch campaign for Aura Smart Home."
- **Screen Focus**: Point out the persistent `[DEMO DATA · READ-ONLY PREVIEW]` banner at the top, showing pre-populated campaign state.

---

### Minute 1:00 - 2:15 — Discovery & Multi-Factor Scoring (Step 3)
- **Action**: Click **Step 3: Creators** in the left sidebar or top stepper.
- **Narration**:
  > "CCIA discovered and analyzed 8 creators in our niche. Notice how James Coffee Tech is ranked #1 with a 92 Fit Score. Unlike basic follower counts, CCIA computes 9 distinct quantitative and qualitative metrics: engagement rate, channel age, upload consistency, niche alignment, tone fit, and brand safety. Notice how Gadget Zone Unboxing was penalized down to a 52 score due to low engagement and spammy ad history."
- **Click**: Click on **James Coffee Tech** card to reveal full radar chart and justifications.

---

### Minute 2:15 - 3:30 — Pre-Mortem Risk Simulation & Lineup Swap (Step 4)
- **Action**: Click **Step 4: Pre-Mortem** in the sidebar.
- **Narration**:
  > "Here is where the magic happens. Before spending a single dollar on contracts, we run a Pre-Mortem simulation. Look at Run #1: our initial health score was **58 out of 100 (High Risk)**. Why? Our pairwise overlap engine detected that James Coffee Tech and Tech & Brews Daily share **48% of the exact same audience**, wasting $4,200 in duplicate ad impressions. Plus, Tech & Brews recently promoted a direct competitor, Flair GO."
- **Action**: Click on **Run #2 (Optimized Lineup)** in the run history tab.
- **Narration**:
  > "CCIA recommended swapping Tech & Brews Daily for Nomad Espresso Guide. Watch how the health score jumped from **58 to 84 (Ready to Launch)**, increasing deduplicated reach to 728,000 unique viewers with zero competitor conflicts!"

---

### Minute 3:30 - 4:30 — Creator Briefs & Compliance Review (Step 5 & 6)
- **Action**: Click **Step 5: Creator Briefs**, then click **Step 6: Compliance**.
- **Narration**:
  > "For each approved creator, CCIA generates a tailored brief with UTM links and mandatory disclosures. When James Coffee Tech submitted his draft video, our compliance engine ran an automated audit. Draft v1 was flagged as **NEEDS REVISION** because it omitted the mandatory `#ad` disclosure hashtag. Once James fixed the description, Draft v2 was marked **APPROVED**."

---

### Minute 4:30 - 5:30 — Google AI Max Search Capture (Step 7)
- **Action**: Click **Step 7: Search Capture**.
- **Narration**:
  > "When creator videos drop, organic search demand spikes. CCIA prepares Google AI Max Search campaign packs—including high-intent query clusters, SEO title formulas, and thumbnail hooks—to capture buyers when they search for 'Aura Smart Espresso Engine review'."

---

### Minute 5:30 - 6:30 — Live Pulse Dashboard (Step 8)
- **Action**: Click **Step 8: Live Pulse**.
- **Narration**:
  > "Once videos drop, CCIA monitors views and sentiment every 15 minutes. Look at James Coffee Tech’s performance: views reached **142,000 in 72 hours—167% of expected median performance**. An automated alert fired recommending: *'Boost Google AI Max search ad budget by $1,500 to capture surging search demand.'*"

---

### Minute 6:30 - 7:00 — Campaign Executive Report (Step 9)
- **Action**: Click **Step 9: Report**.
- **Narration**:
  > "Finally, the Campaign Executive Report synthesizes the entire campaign journey—brief goals, creator lineup scores, Pre-Mortem health score improvement, compliance audit verdicts, search capture strategy, and live pulse performance. It supports 1-click Markdown export and print-friendly PDF layouts. Thank you!"
